How to Play

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I control the paddle?

Drag your finger on the screen to move the paddle.

What if an ad does not load?

Ad availability may vary depending on your network connection and device settings. If a rewarded ad cannot be displayed, the game is designed to allow you to continue without watching the ad.

How can I manage ad privacy choices?

Where available, use “Ad Privacy Settings” in the game to review your advertising privacy choices.

How do I change the tracking permission?

On your iPhone, open Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, then turn pxBREAK on or off. If pxBREAK is not listed, the permission prompt has not been shown yet.

Why do I see ads in the game?

pxBREAK shows banner ads and optional rewarded ads. For details on the data used for ads, see the Privacy Policy.

The game is slow or behaves strangely. What can I do?

  1. Close the game completely and open it again.
  2. Restart your iPhone.
  3. Update iOS and pxBREAK to the latest versions.

If the problem continues, contact us using the email link below.

Contact Support

For questions, bug reports, or feedback, send us an email.

When reporting an issue, please include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The pxBREAK version (shown in the App Store)
  • What you were doing when the problem happened
  • A screenshot, if you have one

We read every message and reply as soon as we can. You can write to us in English or Japanese.
