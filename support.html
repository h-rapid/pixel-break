Frequently Asked Questions

How do I control the paddle?

Drag your finger on the screen to move the paddle.

What if an ad does not load?

Ad availability may vary depending on your network connection and device settings. If a rewarded ad cannot be displayed, the game is designed to allow you to continue without watching the ad.

How can I manage ad privacy choices?

Where available, use “Ad Privacy Settings” in the game to review your advertising privacy choices.
